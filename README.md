## Candidate Skill Match

[![Candidate Skill Match](https://img.shields.io/badge/Candidate_Skill_Match-Upload_Resume-1f3057?style=for-the-badge&logo=googledrive&logoColor=white&labelColor=1f3057)](https://vonnerco.github.io/Tech-Matrix/candidate-match.html)
<br><br>_Upload a candidate's resume and score their fit for the role out of 10._
<br><br>_One at a time, or a whole batch — 50–300 applicants ranked best-first. Large batches run on the server and keep going if you close the tab._
<br><br>_Three simple steps to check the fit_.

1. _Open the matcher._
2. _Upload the candidate's resume as PDF or DOCX — or switch to Batch and drop many._
3. _Get the candidate's skill match score out of 10._

## Build a Tailored Resume

[![Build a Tailored Resume](https://img.shields.io/badge/Build_a_Tailored_Resume-1_keyword-1f3057?style=for-the-badge&logo=googledocs&logoColor=white&labelColor=1f3057)](https://vonnerco.github.io/Tech-Matrix/)
<br><br>Leave the keyword blank to download the original.
<br><br>_Three simple steps to download a new resume_.

1. _Open the generator._
2. _Type one keyword (e.g. Snowflake Data Engineer)._
3. _Download it as PDF or DOCX._

**Job titles rewritten to match the keyword** while **Companies and dates stay the same**
<br> **PDF** (view & send)
<br> **DOCX** (edit to pass ATS)
