FAQ

  • What counts as a screen?

    One scored resume against one job order. A 40-candidate batch is 40 screens.

  • Does the client see the candidate's contact info?

    No — contact details are omitted from submittals by default so clients can't bypass your agency.

  • Is there a contract?

    Month-to-month; cancel anytime.
